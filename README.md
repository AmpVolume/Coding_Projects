# Coding Projects

Welcome to my collection of coding projects! This repository serves as a general archive of the various projects I've worked on throughout my learning and development journey.

The contents include a mixture of:

- **Coursework:** Assignments, exercises, and projects completed as part of my education and technical training.
- **Personal Projects:** Experiments, prototypes, and independent projects created to develop my programming skills and explore new technologies.
- **Client Work:** Projects and development work completed for clients, where sharing is permitted.
- **Miscellaneous Projects:** Small experiments, practice programs, code samples, and other projects that don't necessarily belong in a dedicated repository.

## A Note to Employers and Recruiters

**This repository is not intended to serve as my primary professional portfolio.** It is more of a digital drawer of miscellaneous projects, containing a variety of work at different stages of development, including coursework, experiments, unfinished projects, and smaller exercises.

For that reason, I generally wouldn't recommend browsing this repository as the primary representation of my skills or professional capabilities. Some projects are intended for learning and experimentation rather than production use.

However, you are absolutely welcome to explore the repository if you're interested in seeing more of my coding history, learning process, and the variety of technologies I've worked with.

For a more focused representation of my abilities, I recommend reviewing my dedicated portfolio projects and their individual repositories.

Thanks for stopping by!
